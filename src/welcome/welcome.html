🛡️ Privacy promise

No prompt content ever leaves your browser. Not for debugging. Not for support. Not ever.

Both regex and ML detection run 100% on-device. The ML model weights are bundled in the extension — no downloads, no remote inference, no telemetry by default. You must explicitly opt in to share anonymized threat-intel signals.

Opting in to threat intel (optional)

If you opt in, the Lens sends a small, anonymous "I detected X category Y" signal back to AegisGate. This helps the AegisGate Gateway become smarter over time. The signal contains:

  • A hashed version of the page domain (e.g. a3f1c8d2b9e07456) — not the URL, not the path, not any page content
  • The category that was detected (e.g. pii_email or ml_adversarial_prompt) — not the value
  • The severity and your action (send, redact, cancel)
